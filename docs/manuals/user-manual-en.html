Administrator user guide

Initial setup, account approval and secure sign-in

This guide covers the current administrator entry workflow. Includes manager submissions and administrative report review.

01 · Sign in

  1. Open the portal login page. Choose English or العربية using the language control.
  2. Enter your assigned email address and password, then select Login.
  3. Use your own approved account. An Admin or Department Manager who registered publicly must wait for an existing Admin to approve access.
  4. After login, the portal opens the workspace allowed by your role.

Application screen rendered with isolated local data; not a production capture.

If repeated attempts are blocked, wait at least one minute before retrying. If you forgot your password, use Forgot password instead of repeated guesses.

02 · Prepare departments

  1. As Admin, open Departments from the sidebar. An empty list means no departments have been created in this workspace.
  2. Use the create-department action and complete the required name and any relevant description, then save.
  3. Open Users and edit the relevant employee or manager to assign the department. Save your changes.
  4. A Department Manager manages their assigned department; employees consume its published documents and training.

Application screen rendered with isolated local data; not a production capture.

Financial report submission is a separate Admin-granted permission. Do not grant it to every manager.

03 · Assign properties

  1. Open Properties and create the required property using the available form.
  2. Enter the property details and save. Use the property or user edit screen to link the appropriate landlords.
  3. A landlord may have several properties; a property may have several landlords. Double-check assignments before saving.
  4. For authorized reporting heads, edit their user account, assign their department, enable financial report submission and select their reporting properties.

Application screen rendered with isolated local data; not a production capture.

Reporting permission allows private submission for assigned properties. It does not give a manager publication rights or access to landlord records.

04 · Review account requests

  1. Open Account approval requests under People & access.
  2. Check the applicant’s identity and requested role. An Admin account grants full administrative access.
  3. Where needed, use Edit to assign the manager’s department. Financial reporting permission is configured separately.
  4. Approve an eligible application or reject it. Approved applicants can return to login; rejected applicants remain blocked.

Application screen rendered with isolated local data; not a production capture.

No pending rows means there are currently no applications to review. A recorded decision cannot simply be submitted again.

05 · Password recovery & help

  1. On the login page, choose Forgot password and enter your email address.
  2. The same confirmation is displayed to protect account privacy. It does not confirm that an account exists.
  3. For a registered address, a reset link is sent through the background mail service. Open the link and enter and confirm a new password.
  4. If the message does not arrive, check spam and contact your system administrator. Delivery requires the mail service and queue worker to be configured.
  5. If a department or property is inaccessible, ask the administrator to check your role and assignments. Never share your password or reset link.
Version scope: current entry workflows only. Report review is available; file processing and email require running workers. This guide does not certify production readiness.

06 · Account access & audit

  1. Open Users and edit the account. Scroll to Account access and recovery.
  2. Confirm with your own administrator password. Suspend blocks access and preserves records. Restore removes suspension without bypassing approval.
  3. Send password reset link queues a private email. The account holder chooses their password.
  4. Open Audit logs, select Account and content changes, then Search. Expand Details to inspect before/after values and assignments.

07 · Manager workspace

  1. Your Admin assigns your department and financial reporting permission where needed.
  2. Use Manage content for department documents, training and announcements. New files stay hidden until background processing completes.
  3. Admins can archive or restore departments and properties on their edit screens. Only unused archived records can be deleted.

08 · Submit a monthly report

  1. Select an assigned active property and month. Attach PDF or Excel, enter figures, then save a draft or submit.
  2. File processing must succeed before approval. Spreadsheet figures are not extracted automatically.
  3. Pending reports are locked. Read review comments on returned reports, correct and resubmit. Approved and rejected reports remain locked.

09 · Review and publish

  1. Open Financial report review in the Admin navigation. Filter by report status.
  2. Check the manager, figures and attachment. Return or reject with a reason, or approve after processing succeeds.
  3. Approval publishes the report to assigned landlords and queues notifications. Existing property/month reports cannot be overwritten.
  4. Review history and reviewed original attachments are retained. Approved management figures appear separately from detailed financial breakdowns.
